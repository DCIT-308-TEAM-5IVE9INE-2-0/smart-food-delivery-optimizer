## Purpose

Meeting 3 is the first progress-review meeting after the technical project scaffold was created. The meeting should confirm that all teams understand the current repository setup, review what each team has done so far, correct the team/member alignment records, and assign the next concrete milestone tasks.

## Pre-Meeting Requirements

Before the meeting, every member should:

- Send their GitHub username to the Technical Lead.
- Confirm they can access the private repository after being added.
- Review the current project scaffold.
- Check the folder or document related to their team.
- Prepare a short update on what they have completed so far.
- Prepare one question, challenge or contribution for discussion.
- Review their assigned data structure and algorithm/topic.
